L16: Monthly Returns:   July 1926 - August 2026

L18: Annual Returns:   1927 - 2025

L20: Portfolios:   BE < 0; bottom 30%, middle 40%, top 30%; quintiles; deciles.
L21: Firms with negative book equity are in only the BE < 0 portfolio.
L22: Construction:   Portfolios are formed on BE/ME at the end of each June using NYSE breakpoints. The BE used in June of year t is the book equity for the last fiscal year end in t-1. ME is price times shares outstanding at the end of December of t-1.
L23: 
L24: Stocks:   All NYSE, AMEX, and NASDAQ stocks for which we have ME for December of t-1 and June of t, and BE for t-1.
